The Story

What is Aam Panna?

Aam Panna is a quintessential Indian summer drink made from raw green mangoes. For centuries, it has been the go-to remedy and refresher across South Asia during the scorching summer months — a drink passed down through generations with pride.

The drink is made by roasting or boiling raw mangoes, then blending them with cumin, black salt, sugar, and fresh mint — creating a uniquely tangy, sweet, and deeply cooling beverage unlike anything else.

Jahnvi's homemade Aam Panna is crafted in small batches with zero artificial ingredients, honouring the original recipe with full heart and care.
